Widgetify provides home screen widgets (clocks, battery, weather, calculator, timer, notes, games and more). This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and who it is shared with. We do not require an account and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Data stored on your device only

Widget settings, widget state (for example game boards, notes, habits, timers, countdowns), your theme choices and your premium status are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Data sent to third parties

  • Weather, quotes and sports data providers: when you use these widgets, the app sends the request needed to fetch the data (for example an approximate location or a city name for weather, a league or team name for sports). We do not attach your name or any account to these requests.
  • Advertising (free version): the free version displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings. Users of the paid Premium version do not see ads.
  • Payments: the one-time Premium purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts and do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Contacts, screen time, steps and notes are never transmitted to us.

5. Children

Widgetify is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data retention and your rights

Because data is stored on your device, you control it: you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, Switzerland or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data on our servers, requests mostly concern ad data handled by Google, which you can manage in your Google account settings. You can also contact us at the email above.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Network requests use HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, inform users in the app or in the Play Store listing.
